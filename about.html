FAQ

Common Questions

How does the domain transfer work?

Once payment is secured in escrow, we initiate a domain push to your registrar account (GoDaddy, Namecheap, Cloudflare, etc.). This typically takes minutes to a few hours. After you confirm receipt, escrow releases the funds to us.

Can I negotiate the price?

Yes. Every domain has a listed price and a "make an offer" floor. Serious offers within range are always considered. Bulk inquiries for multiple domains receive preferential pricing.

What payment methods are accepted?

Escrow.com accepts wire transfer, credit card, and PayPal (for qualifying transactions). Dan.com supports credit card, PayPal, and wire transfer. Both platforms handle the financial protection.

Do you offer payment plans?

Dan.com offers a Lease-to-Own option on select domains, allowing you to pay in monthly installments while using the domain immediately. Contact us to discuss eligibility.

What is included besides the domain?

Every acquisition includes: (1) Full domain ownership transfer, (2) A Brand Vision Document outlining positioning and audience strategy, (3) A Strategic Logo Concept for immediate brand development.

Are these domains trademarked?

Our domains are brandable digital assets. They are not registered trademarks. The acquiring party is responsible for trademark registration in their jurisdiction. We recommend consulting a trademark attorney before filing.
